The Music Editor

Everything is a gesture. Draw on the page below with a mouse, a pen or a finger. There are no menus: the marks on the page bid for each stroke and the best bid wins. An upward stroke anywhere is undo.

Actions

Page

  • W-Wfirst stroke: sets the top staff line. Then, below the first system: add a staff to it
  • W-Eleft, then back right (a retrace) below the last system: add a new system

Bars

  • S-Stop line to bottom line of a staff: barline. On a bar: cycle single, double, fine
  • S-Sbottom line of one staff to top line of the next: join the barlines across the gap
  • DOTclick left/right of a bar: repeat dots on that side

Notes

  • SW-SWa short down-left tick on a line or space: note head
  • S-Sjust right of a head: up stem; just left: down stem; through stemmed heads: unstem
  • E-Eacross a stem: add a flag; across two stems: beam them; across a beamed group: add a beam
  • W-Wacross a stem: remove a flag (quarter, half, whole)
  • DOTclick after a head: augmentation dot (cycles 0 to 3)

Rests

  • W-Sleft then down: quarter rest
  • E-Sright then down (a 7): eighth rest
  • E-E / W-Wacross a rest: more / fewer flags. DOT: dot it

Clefs and keys

  • SW-SEa < from the top line to the bottom line: G clef
  • SE-SWa > from the top line to the bottom line: F clef
  • E-E / W-Wacross the left margin: sharps / flats in the key. Across a double bar: key change

Undo

  • N-Nan upward stroke anywhere

Shape database

Gestures are recognized against trained prototypes. Train shapes lets you add your own examples (type a name like S-S, draw it a few times, press Enter to save in this browser).
